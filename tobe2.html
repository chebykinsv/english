To Be — теория и практика

90 фраз · теория, тренажёр и экзамен

📌 Формы глагола to be

I am → I'm

He / She / It is → 's

We / You / They are → 're

Отрицание: isn't / aren't / I'm not.

Вопрос: глагол to be ставим в начало — Is he busy? Are they here?

📌 Примеры с to be

This video is very informative.

These videos are very informative.

All these videos are very useful.

All your videos are so interesting.

This student is so hard-working.

These students are very lazy.

My boss is very busy now.

Her boss is very busy at the moment.

📌 Единственное и множественное число

man — men · woman — women

This man is a doctor. — These men are managers.

That woman is a designer. — Those women are teachers.

These lessons are so effective.

Those lessons are ineffective (those lessons aren't effective).

This problem is very big. — These problems are small.

this / that → единственное число · these / those → множественное.

📌 Короткие фразы, которые стоит запомнить

Time is money.

Money is important.

English is easy.

Chinese is very difficult.

Love is a wonderful feeling.

Knowledge is power.

This place is very beautiful.

📌 Возраст

I'm 21 (years old) — twenty-one.

I'm 32 (years old) — thirty-two.

Возраст в английском — через to be, а не «have»: He's 32. My brother is 21. His wife is 32.

Вопрос о возрасте: I'm thirty-one. And you?
